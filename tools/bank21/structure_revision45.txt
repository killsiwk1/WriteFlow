E|The broken railing needs to be replace before the tour.|replace|replaced|游览前需要更换损坏的栏杆。|不定式的被动|栏杆承受更换这个动作，to be 后用过去分词 replaced。|不能在 be 后用 replace 原形组成被动。

E|The photograph reminded me for the day we moved.|for|of|这张照片让我想起搬家的那一天。|remind somebody of|remind me of 引出被唤起的记忆。|remind somebody to do 表示提醒某人做事，结构不同。
E|Our question is if or not the roof can carry the panels.|if|whether|我们的问题是屋顶能否承受这些面板。|whether or not|直接与 or not 相连时用 whether，后接陈述语序。|不能写 if or not。
E|The road was closed because of a tree had fallen across it.|because of|because|因为一棵树倒在了路中间，道路封闭了。|because 与 because of|a tree had fallen 是完整从句，需要连词 because。|because of 后接名词短语，不直接接完整分句。
E|I would rather stayed here until the fog lifts.|stayed|stay|我宁愿留在这里，直到雾散去。|would rather do|would rather 后面直接接动词原形。|此处没有另一个主语，不用 would rather somebody did 的过去式。
E|The visitor insisted on see the original drawing.|see|seeing|这位访客坚持要看原图。|介词后的动名词|insist on 中 on 是介词，后接 seeing 作宾语。|不定式 to see 不能直接跟在 on 后面。
X|I last visited the island three years ago.|从 I haven't 开头，用 for three years 表示这段时间一次也没去。|I haven't visited the island for three years./I have not visited the island for three years.|我已经三年没去过那座岛了。|上一次经历与现在完成时否定|三年前是最后一次，表示从那以后到现在都没有再去。|不能把 last 和 for three years 简单放在同一过去时句中。
X|The teacher let us use the workshop after lunch.|从 We were allowed 开头改写，保留 after lunch。|We were allowed to use the workshop after lunch.|午饭后，我们获准使用工坊。|let 与 allow 的转换|主动 let us use 可改为 we were allowed to use。|被动 be allowed 后的不定式需要 to。
X|Although the sign was clear, two visitors missed the turn.|以 Despite the clear sign 开头改写，保留主句。|Despite the clear sign, two visitors missed the turn.|尽管指示牌很清楚，两名游客还是错过了转弯处。|让步从句改为介词短语|Despite 后面接名词短语 the clear sign。|不要写 despite of。
X|The museum will display the repaired boat next week.|从 The repaired boat 开头改被动，使用 by the museum。|The repaired boat will be displayed by the museum next week./The repaired boat will be displayed next week by the museum.|修复好的船将在下周由博物馆展出。|将来时被动|will 后接 be，display 变为过去分词 displayed。|不能缺少被动助动词 be。
X|Nia finished the drawing before she sent the email.|以 After finishing the drawing 开头，主句要明确写 Nia。|After finishing the drawing, Nia sent the email.|完成画作之后，妮娅发出了邮件。|共同主语下的 -ing 短语|前后动作都由 Nia 执行，可将前一动作压缩为 After finishing...。|若主句主语不是画画的人，便会使分词指向错误。
X|Someone has already repaired the yellow bench.|从 The yellow bench 开头改为现在完成时被动，保留 already。|The yellow bench has already been repaired./The yellow bench has been repaired already.|那张黄色长椅已经修好了。|完成时被动语态|has been repaired 同时表示完成和被动。|不要只写 has repaired，长椅并非修理者。
X|“Did you lock the side gate?” the coach asked me.|从 The coach asked me 开头，用 whether 或 if，按过去完成时转述。|The coach asked me whether I had locked the side gate./The coach asked me if I had locked the side gate.|教练问我是否锁上了侧门。|转述一般疑问句|whether 或 if 引导是否；I had locked 表示先锁门后提问。|从句内不能保留 Did you lock 的语序。
X|You must not open the cover while the machine is running.|从 The cover 开头改为含 must 的被动句。|The cover must not be opened while the machine is running./The cover mustn't be opened while the machine is running.|机器运行时严禁打开盖子。|情态动词的被动|must not 后放 be opened，保留禁止的强度。|need not 表示没必要，并非禁止。
X|We found the trail because Hana had marked the rocks.|用 Without Hana's marks on the rocks 开头，主句用 would not have found the trail。|Without Hana's marks on the rocks, we would not have found the trail./Without Hana's marks on the rocks, we wouldn't have found the trail.|要不是哈娜留在岩石上的标记，我们就找不到小径了。|介词短语表达过去假设|Without 引出缺失条件，主句用 would have 加过去分词。|假设结果与实际已经找到小径的事实相反。
X|The last bus had left before we reached the stop.|以 When we reached the stop 开头，主句使用 already。|When we reached the stop, the last bus had already left./When we reached the stop, the last bus had left already.|我们到车站时，末班车已经开走了。|重排过去动作的先后|从句提供过去参照点，更早的离开用 had left。|不要改变哪一个动作先发生。
X|I do not know how I should label the samples.|把 how 从句改为 how 加不定式，保留 I do not know。|I do not know how to label the samples./I don't know how to label the samples.|我不知道该如何给样品贴标签。|疑问词加不定式|主句和从句的动作执行者相同，可简化为 how to label。|不要同时保留 I should 与 to。
X|The bicycle belongs to the nurse who lives next door.|改用所有格；从 The bicycle is the 开头，先用 next-door 作定语。|The bicycle is the next-door nurse's.|这辆自行车是住在隔壁的护士的。|名词所有格独立使用|nurse's 可单独表示“护士的自行车”，无需重复 bicycle。|所有格撇号在单数 nurse 的 s 前。
